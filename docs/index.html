Central idea

Emotional coordination depends on what is happening and who is talking.

TRACE treats a conversation as an ordered interaction trace rather than a bag of utterances. It tracks affect across alternating speaker windows and conditions that evidence on the conversational situation and the speakers' relationship.

Method

From two speech channels to an interaction trace.

TRACE extracts affect-aware Whisper embeddings from fixed windows, interleaves the two speakers in temporal order, and integrates global conversational context and relationship information.

01Window speech

Aligned 3-second windows from both speakers.

02Encode affect

Emotion-finetuned Whisper-large-v3 representations.

03Model interaction

Alternating dyadic sequence with bidirectional temporal blocks.

04Condition socially

SBERT context and relationship embeddings via late fusion or cross-attention.

DyadEE benchmark

A controlled testbed for disrupted emotional coordination.

DyadEE is built from Seamless Interaction. Positive examples preserve emotionally entrained interaction; negative examples use emotion-contradicting resynthesis to break inter-speaker affective coordination. Voice conversion reduces reliance on superficial synthesis artifacts.

Abstract

Relationship- and context-aware emotional entrainment detection.

With the proliferation of speech AI agents, understanding emotional entrainment in conversational interaction has become increasingly important. Emotional entrainment is shaped by social relationships and conversational context, influencing affective coordination over time. We introduce DyadEE, a dataset containing emotionally entrained conversations and controlled interactions where entrainment is disrupted through emotion resynthesis.

We further propose TRACE, a window-level framework that models dyadic interaction as ordered sequences of acoustic embeddings derived from emotion-finetuned Whisper representations. On DyadEE, relationship and context information improve emotional entrainment detection, with TRACE achieving 93.47% accuracy.
